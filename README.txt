BESTEP Self-Practice Center — Final Candidate

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- One mobile-friendly website.
- Listening: 5 complete practice sets, each 35 questions (10 + 10 + 15).
- Reading: 5 complete practice sets, each 35 questions (5 + 15 + 15).
  * Reading passages appear once per question set, followed by the related questions.
- Speaking: 5 complete practice sets.
  * Part 1: 6 questions, each played twice, 15 seconds to answer, no restart.
  * Part 2: chart + 3 questions, 1.5 min preparation + 1.5 min response.
  * Part 3: passage + chart + 2 instructions, 2.5 min preparation + 2.5 min response.
  * Tips and sample responses appear after practice; optional AI-feedback prompt is provided.
- Writing: 5 complete practice sets.
  * Part 1: 3 short-answer questions.
  * Part 2: about 80 words, with varied email/message/opinion situations.
  * Part 3: integrated writing, about 120–150 words.
  * Students write on paper or in their own document. No answer is submitted to this site.
  * Answer guides, sample responses, and optional AI-feedback prompts are provided.

PRIVACY / COST
- No login.
- No database.
- No student data storage.
- No OpenAI or Gemini API.
- No AI grading fee.

AUDIO
This downloadable version uses the best English speech voice available on each student's browser/device.
Because browser/device voices differ, identical studio-quality audio cannot be guaranteed in this offline package.
The site structure allows prerecorded MP3 files to replace the speech source later without rewriting the test bank.

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Double-click index.html.
3. Use Chrome.
